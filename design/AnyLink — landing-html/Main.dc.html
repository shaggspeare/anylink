How it works

From a pile
to a library.

Three steps, no filing required. AnyLink does the sorting so you can get back to the good stuff.

01

Save

Paste any link, or import your browser bookmarks and Telegram saved messages in one go.

  • Title, image, price and read time pulled for you
  • Blocked pages get a fallback read, not a dead row
02

Organise

Links land in collections by what they actually are — things to buy, to read, to try.

  • Dead links flagged before they pile up
  • Trash keeps your mistakes recoverable
03

Find

One query language behind search, filters and saved views. Learn it once, use it everywhere.

  • Searches the page text, not just titles
  • Save the searches you repeat as smart collections
